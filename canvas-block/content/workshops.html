Small-group sessions in the studio, most Saturday mornings from spring to late autumn.

What's on

  • Hand-tied bouquets - two hours, beginners welcome
  • Seasonal wreaths - November and December only
  • Table centrepieces - ideal for events and gatherings

What to expect

Groups are capped at eight so there is plenty of time for one-to-one help. All flowers, tools and refreshments are included.
