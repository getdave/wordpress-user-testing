Bluebell & Bloom is a two-person flower studio working with seasonal, locally grown stems wherever we can.

How we work

We started out selling bunches from a market stall. Today we have a small studio, but the idea is the same. Buy what is in season, waste as little as possible, and let the flowers lead the design.

Most of our stems come from growers within an hour of the studio. In winter we lean on dried flowers and foliage rather than imports.
